OVERVIEW

An Ed-Tech Start-UP Website

Tech Skool is an EdTech startup teaching digital skills to students across Nigeria. During my internship, I worked across marketing and product, redesigning parts of the website and building a lightweight assessment portal to fix a real problem of consistent and intuitive evaluation methods for students.

Problem

Tech Skool had no website. Students were assessed through a Google Form, and completion rates were low. A generic form gave no feedback, no login, no way to check standing, and no sense of progress, so students became disengaged. This was quietly costing Tech Skool visibility into who was actually learning.

Opportunity

A chance to build something for Tech Skool to have presence online and an assessment portal students would actually finish. A "tap and go" experience that felt like part of the product, not an external chore.

WEBSITE DESIGN

High converting Landing Page

For an Ed tech start up it's important to have a clear About us and Social Proof. The users should know clearly know what you do and if they can trust you to be a valuable source of knowledge.
